# ADR 0027: The Monthly Pack, one place for the month's statutory work

Status: accepted for design (Owner chose it, 2026-11-14: "1"). Owner of this ADR: Sigma. Work order for Brave: `docs/phase7b-plan-sigma.md`.

**Why.** After payroll is approved, an Associate repeats the same chores for every client: the EPF file and payment, the ESIC sheet and contribution, the tax deducted from salary, professional tax where a state levies it, labour welfare in its months. Today each is a separate screen, and nothing says what is still to do, when it is due, or what was filed. With 50 companies that is the month's real work. The old applications of the same kind are slow here; the Monthly Pack is where the new one is meant to be faster and to keep the records.

**Decision.**
1. **A Monthly Pack screen for the open company and a month** (default: last month), under Compliance > Monthly. One line per statutory item that applies to that company: EPF, ESIC, professional tax (one line for each state that levies it), TDS, labour welfare (only in the months its rule falls due). Each line shows the status, the amount payable, the number of employees and the due date.
2. **Statuses:** Not ready (the month's payroll is not approved or locked, or something is missing, with the reason), Ready, Generated, Filed, Paid, and Changed (generated before, but the figures changed since, for example after a payroll reopen).
3. **One click:** "Generate all ready" writes every file and a summary workbook into a folder the user chooses (default `Documents\Revolution\<company>\<yyyy-MM>`). One line can also be generated alone. The files are the ones the app already makes (the EPFO ECR file, the ESIC upload sheet) plus a summary sheet for professional tax, TDS and labour welfare. Nothing is uploaded to a portal. An existing file is never overwritten: the new one gets a number.
4. **Records.** The Associate marks a line Filed or Paid with the reference (acknowledgement or challan number) and the date. These are kept per company and month, so "what was filed for Acme in October, and when" has an answer for years.
5. **Figures come only from the approved or locked payroll of the month.** The existing rule for statutory exports stands: plain ids are decrypted only for an approved or locked run, and every export is logged first (ADR 0019).
6. **Due dates are data, not code,** in `stat.DueDateRule` (effective-dated, with an `IsVerified` flag like the other statutory rates). A line whose rule is not verified shows "Date not verified" instead of a date. Proposed rules for the CA to verify: provident fund and state insurance contributions by the 15th of the next month; tax deducted from salary by the 7th of the next month, and 30 April for March; professional tax and labour welfare from the state tables (`stat.PtDueMonth`, `stat.LabourWelfareRule`); the Haryana labour welfare due date and wage definition are still open (part G of the CA pack).
7. **Which lines appear** follows the plan and the company: EPF and ESIC need `PlanFeature.EpfEsi`; professional tax needs `ProfessionalTax` and a state with an active rule; TDS needs `Tds`; labour welfare needs a rule for the company's state. A company on a lower plan sees only what its plan has.
8. **Phase B, later: the Associate's overview.** One table of all the Associate's companies for a month (company by item, with status and due date, urgent ones first), opened from the company switcher. Row-level security lets a connection read one company at a time, so the overview opens one scoped connection for each company the Associate works on; Sigma builds that (a company-scoped connection factory, with the access check the company switcher already makes) after Phase A. Target: 50 companies in under 3 seconds.

**Shared code (Sigma, done with this ADR).** `Revolution.Core`: `ComplianceKind`, `PackStatus`, `PackAmount`, `MonthlyPackLine`, `MonthlyPack`, `IMonthlyPackService` and `MonthlyPackSheetBuilder` (the summary workbook), with tests. Sigma builds the screens (`MonthlyPackForm`, later the overview) and the menu entry once Brave's service exists.

**Not decided here.** Direct portal upload (files only for now). Challan forms and the annual returns (Form 24Q, Form 16): the Monthly Pack lists their amounts but does not make them. Errors 53650 to 53699 are reserved for the Monthly Pack.
