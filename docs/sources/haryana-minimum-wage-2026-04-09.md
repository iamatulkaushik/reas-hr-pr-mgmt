# Haryana minimum wages from 1 April 2026

Source: Haryana Government Gazette Extraordinary No. 51-2026/Ext., 9 April 2026, Labour Department Notification No. 2/25/26-2 Lab (file: haryana-minimum-wage-2026-04-09.pdf). Issued under section 8(3) of the Code on Wages, 2019. Replaces Notification 3/42/83-3 Lab of 21 October 2015. Applies to all employments in Haryana; brick kilns are notified separately.

| Category | Per month | Per day |
|---|---|---|
| Unskilled | 15220.71 | 585.41 |
| Semi-Skilled | 16780.74 | 645.41 |
| Skilled | 18500.81 | 711.56 |
| Highly-Skilled | 19425.85 | 747.14 |

Notes that matter for the software (as notified):
- Basic rates; they may not be split into allowances. The consumer price index linkage is neutralised, so the wage does not fall below these rates.
- Per day wage = monthly wage / 26. A deduction for absence = monthly wage / 30 (or as the government prescribes).
- A trainee gets 75% of the category rate, never less than the unskilled rate, for at most one year. Apprentices fall under the Apprentices Act, 1961.
- No difference between men and women. The principal employer is responsible for contractors' workers.
- The notification has one zone and four skill levels (no zone split).
