# Haryana Labour Welfare Fund

Source: Haryana Labour Welfare Board page https://hrylabour.gov.in/content/cms/MTU (read 2026-10-24). Act: The Punjab Labour Welfare Fund Act, 1965, section 9-A. Applies to employees of industrial and commercial establishments in Haryana.

| Effective | Employee (per month) | Employer (per month) |
|---|---|---|
| January 2002 | Rs 1 | Rs 2 |
| April 2007 | Rs 5 | Rs 10 |
| April 2012 | Rs 10 | Rs 20 |
| April 2019 | 0.2% of salary, wages or remuneration, at most Rs 25 | Twice the employee's amount |

Not on the page, so not loaded: the due date and how often it is paid, the wage definition, which establishments are covered, and the wage cutoff. The CA confirms these before the rule is verified.
